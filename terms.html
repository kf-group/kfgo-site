Bookings

A booking is confirmed only when kfGo confirms it on WhatsApp, email or phone after payment (or, for multi-day trips, after the advance). Prices on the website are in Indian rupees and are for the services described on each trip page.

Prices and what they include

  • Shared day tours are priced per seat. Private trips, airport transfers and cabs are priced per vehicle.
  • Vehicle prices include the vehicle, driver, fuel and driver allowance for the planned route. Tolls, parking, entry tickets, meals, stays and activities are extra unless the trip page says otherwise.
  • Extra kilometres, hours or stops requested during the trip are charged at the rate card.

Payment

Pay by UPI or the online payment options shown at checkout, quoting your booking reference. For multi-day trips, the advance confirms the booking and the balance is due before the trip starts.
